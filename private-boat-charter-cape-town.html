The boat

Wu Wei is a sports cruiser with a wide sun pad up front, a shaded cockpit, a swim platform at the back with a ladder and fresh-water rinse, and a cabin below for changing or hiding from the wind. Big enough to spread out, small enough to anchor in close.

Shape your day

Every charter is private and quoted for your group. Head round to Clifton and Camps Bay, stay in Table Bay with the mountain behind you, or stay out for the sunset. Tell us what you're celebrating and we'll plan it with you.

What's included

Your skipper, fuel, food and standard drinks. Want something special? Ask when you enquire, or bring your own drinks.

Booking

Send an enquiry or a WhatsApp and you get a quote for your group within the day. Full payment confirms your date. If the weather turns, we move it at no cost.
