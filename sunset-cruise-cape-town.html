Sundowner cruise

One hour on the water in the late afternoon, in the bay or round to Sea Point and back, as Table Mountain turns gold. Drinks and snacks included.

How it goes

  1. Meet us at Granger Bay in the late afternoon for a quick safety briefing.
  2. Head out into Table Bay, or round to Sea Point and back.
  3. Drinks and snacks in hand as the light goes gold over the mountain.
  4. Back at the harbour two hours later.

Good for

A first evening in Cape Town, a date, a birthday drink, or showing visitors the city from the water. It's private, so it's just your group on board.

What to bring

A warm layer. It cools down quickly on the water once the sun drops.

Weather

Skipper B calls it on the day. If the wind is up, we move your date at no cost.

Book your day

The sundowner cruise is R7,000 for two hours. That is for the whole boat, up to 12 guests, with drinks and snacks included. Want to stay out longer? Each extra hour is R5,000. Send an enquiry and we'll tell you if your date is free.
