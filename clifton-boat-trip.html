Clifton boat trip

Out past Granger Bay and round to the Clifton beaches. Anchor in the clear water, swim, float, lie in the sun. The classic Cape Town day on the water.

How the day goes

  1. Meet us at Granger Bay for a five-minute safety briefing.
  2. Cruise along the Atlantic Seaboard with Table Mountain and Lion's Head behind you.
  3. Drop anchor off Clifton. Swim off the platform, float, or stay on the sun pad.
  4. Food and standard drinks are on board the whole time.
  5. Head home when you're ready.

What's included

Your skipper, fuel, food and standard drinks. Want something special? Ask when you enquire, or bring your own drinks.

What to bring

Swimwear, a towel, sunscreen and a warm layer for the trip home. The Atlantic is cold even in summer, so the brave get the bragging rights.

Weather and safety

Skipper B calls it the morning of the trip. If the wind or swell is up, we move your date at no cost. Wu Wei is permitted for commercial charter, with a licensed skipper, life jackets for everyone (kids' sizes too) and full safety gear on board.

Book your day

The Clifton trip is R25,000 for six hours. That is for the whole boat, up to 12 guests, with food and standard drinks included. Want to stay out longer? Each extra hour is R5,000. Send an enquiry and we'll tell you if your date is free.
