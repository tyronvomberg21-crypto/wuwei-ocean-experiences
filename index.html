Good to know

What to bring
Swimwear, a towel, sunscreen and a warm layer for the trip home. The Atlantic is cold even in summer, so the brave get the bragging rights.
Weather
Skipper B calls it the morning of the trip. If the wind or swell is up, we move your date at no cost. Safety first, every time.
Children
Welcome. Life jackets in kids' sizes are on board, and the swim platform makes getting in and out easy.
Food and drinks
Always supplied. Food and standard drinks are on board for every charter. Want something special? Ask when you enquire. You're welcome to bring your own drinks too.
Seasickness
We stay close to the coast in calm water, so most people are fine. If you're prone to it, take something an hour before.
Safety
Fully permitted and compliant for commercial charter, with a licensed skipper, life jackets for everyone and full safety gear on board.
